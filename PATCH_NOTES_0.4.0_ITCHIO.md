# 🚀 Alpha 0.4.0 Major Update: Aerial Threats, Mutated Enemies & Capsule Loadouts!

Hey everyone! Welcome to **Alpha 0.4.0**! 💥

This update brings huge additions to combat variety, character customization, enemy mechanics, and quality of life. Get ready to rethink your survival strategies as enemies evolve and take to the skies!

Here is everything new in this update:

---

## 🎒 Pre-Game Ability Loadout System
You can now tailor your playstyle **before** jumping into battle!
- **New Loadout Menu**: Access the Loadout panel directly from the Main Menu to choose and equip your favorite **Active Ability Capsule**.
- **Dedicated Skill Key (`Q`)**: Trigger your equipped ability on demand with the **Q** key.
- **On-Screen Cooldowns**: Keep track of your capsule readiness with the new HUD cooldown indicator.
- **Full Ability Rebalance**: All capsule abilities (Healing, Damage, Pull, Push, Stun, Silence, Blind, Slow, and Vulnerability) have been polished with streamlined 5-second cooldowns and clearer tooltips!

---

## 🛸 Look Up! New Flying Enemy
Think you're safe on the ground? Think again!
- **Aerial Menace**: A brand-new flying enemy archetype that hovers above the horde, weaves through the air, and dives straight toward you.
- Don't let them swarm above you while you're busy handling grounded threats!

---

## 🧪 Mutated Enemies: Elemental Status Threats
Surviving past the 1-minute mark just got a lot more dangerous. Enemies can now spawn as **Mutated Variants** with dangerous status effects:
- 🔴 **Damage Boosted (Red)**: Hits way harder than usual. Dodge or eliminate quickly!
- 🔵 **Frost / Slowness (Cyan)**: Hits from these enemies slow your movement down—making you vulnerable to swarms.
- 🟣 **Vulnerability / Weakness (Purple)**: Getting hit makes you take increased damage from all sources for a short time.
- 🟡 **Stun (Gold / Yellow)**: Temporarily paralyses your movement. Stay on your toes!

*Tip: Keep your distance from glowing colored enemies!*

---

## ✨ New Item: Magic Aura Amulet
- **Magic Aura**: Harness arcane energy to slash your ability cooldowns, letting you fire off active skills and capsules much more frequently.

---

## 💥 Combat Polish & Visual Enhancements
- **Red Floating Critical Numbers**: Landing a juicy Critical Hit now pops up with vivid **red damage numbers**, making every crit feel satisfying!
- **Auto-Reload When Empty**: Firing when your clip runs dry will now automatically trigger a weapon reload so you never miss a beat in the middle of a firefight.
- **Enhanced Level & XP Display**: The experience bar at the top of your screen now clearly displays your current **Level (Lvl. X)** and exact XP progress (**current / required**).
- **Clearer Upgrade Selection**: Upgrades on the level-up draft screen now feature crisp color highlighting when selected, preventing accidental picks.

---

## 📋 Full Summary of Changes
- ✨ **New**: Pre-game Loadout System with dedicated Capsule slot.
- ⌨️ **New**: Dedicated Ability keybind (**Q**).
- 🛸 **New**: Flying enemy archetype with dynamic hover flight.
- 🧪 **New**: 4 Enemy Status Effect mutations (Damage, Slowness, Weakness, Stun).
- 📿 **New**: Magic Aura amulet (Cooldown reduction).
- 📊 **New**: XP numerical counters and Level badge on HUD.
- 🎯 **QoL**: Automatic reload when firing an empty weapon.
- 💥 **Visuals**: Vivid red floating numbers on Critical Hits.
- 🎨 **UI**: Selection state highlighting for upgrade cards.
- ⚖️ **Balance**: Standardized capsule cooldowns (5s) and adjusted enemy difficulty scaling over time.
